Delete Your Logmaxxing Account

This page explains how to delete an account for Logmaxxing, listed on Google Play as Logmaxxing - Workout Log.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Logmaxxing and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Workout Settings.
  3. Scroll to the App section at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm the deletion prompt.

In-app deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account and your Firestore account data, including workout logs, weight logs, profile details, exercise definitions, workout-day summaries, and exercise records.

Request deletion from the web

If you cannot access the app, email kunaaldesai13@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, or include that sign-in email in your request. Do not send your password.

We may need to verify that you control the account before completing deletion. Verified deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days unless a longer period is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

What may be retained

Limited backup, security, legal, dispute-resolution, or fraud-prevention records may be retained where required or permitted by law.
